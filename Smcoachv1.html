⚠️ 2 Possible Solutions Before Calling DM

Select Situation

Is the customer demanding a manager or refusing resolution at the counter?

Have you fully followed company policy (returns, warranties, pricing, courtesy steps)?

Did you clearly explain the policy AND offer at least one alternative solution?

Is the customer still escalating AFTER policy + solution + manager involvement?

Do you have a shift uncovered that impacts store operation or coverage?

Have you attempted ALL coverage options (call-ins, schedule adjustments, part-time help)?

Have you adjusted roles (counter to driver, manager to counter, etc.)?

Is the store unable to safely operate or serve customers effectively?

Is the issue preventing sales, checkout, or normal store function?

Have you restarted ALL affected systems (POS, terminals, network)?

Have you contacted helpdesk and followed troubleshooting steps?

Is the issue STILL causing lost sales or inability to operate?

Is there a cash variance that exceeds acceptable limits?

Have you checked all tills, deposits, safe counts, and paperwork?

Is the variance still unexplained and significant?

Is delivery failure impacting commercial customers or sales?

Have you reassigned drivers or adjusted delivery expectations?

Is the issue still causing lost business or major disruption?

Is this a policy you cannot find or confidently interpret?

Have you checked handbook, system, and previous guidance?

Is this decision high-risk (legal, financial, or customer impact)?
